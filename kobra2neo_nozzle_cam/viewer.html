Parts and validation

Checks run on every STL

  • Watertight, one body, flat bed contact
  • Wall thickness by ray cast: min 1.2 mm, 2.0 recommended
  • Features under 0.8 mm (two 0.4 mm lines)
  • Overhangs past 45° from vertical, bridges over 20 mm
  • Part-to-part collision in the assembled pose: none
  • Edges: 1.5 mm fillets on top and vertical edges, 0.5 mm chamfers on bed edges, 3 mm fillets in inside corners. The socket split faces stay sharp so the halves meet flush.

Rules from the flowful-ai cad-skill print defaults. "Wall min" ignores knife edges where a curve meets a flat; p1 is the thinnest 1% of surface.

Cable route

The flat cable leaves the open back of the cradle and goes straight back to the arm as a loose loop across the ball joint. It runs through two clips on the arm, loops past the rosette, and goes through the base clip up to the head's cable bundle. To clip it in, slide the cable in on its edge and turn it flat. Thread the arm clips before bolting the arm on: their openings face the head.
